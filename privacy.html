Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Dekagrophy is the personal photography, videography and design practice of Giancarlo (Deka) Rosete. This policy explains what personal information is collected through this website and through Instagram messages, what it is used for, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted.

Who is responsible

Giancarlo (Deka) Rosete, trading as Dekagrophy, is the data controller for the information described here. For any privacy question or request, contact giancarlorrv@gmail.com.

Information collected through the contact form

When you submit the enquiry form on this website, you choose what to send. The form collects:

  • First and last name, and optionally a preferred name
  • Email address, so a reply can be sent
  • Instagram handle and WhatsApp number, if you provide them
  • Your description of the shoot, and any preferred date and time

This information is sent directly to Dekagrophy's email inbox using EmailJS, a third-party delivery service that transmits the message. It is used solely to answer your enquiry and arrange a booking. No website account is created, and the form does not store your submission in a database on this site.

Information collected through Instagram messages

Dekagrophy operates an automated reply service on the Instagram account @dekagrophy. It exists so that common questions — about pricing, availability and booking — receive an immediate acknowledgement outside working hours.

What it receives

When you send a direct message to @dekagrophy, Meta delivers that message to this service. It receives the text of your message and your Instagram-scoped user ID, which is an identifier issued by Meta and is not your username, your email address or your phone number.

What it does with it

Your message text is checked against a fixed list of keywords in order to select a pre-written reply. If nothing matches, no reply is sent and the message is simply left for a human to read. Messages containing photos, videos, shared reels, story replies and voice notes are ignored entirely.

What it keeps

The service has no database. Your message text is never written to storage — it is examined in memory and discarded.

The only thing retained is your Instagram-scoped user ID together with the time an automated reply was sent, held in temporary memory so that the same person is not answered automatically more than once in a seven-day period. This is erased whenever the service restarts, which happens routinely.

Your messages of course remain in the Instagram inbox itself, as they would with any conversation. Instagram's own handling of that data is governed by Instagram's Privacy Policy.

What is never done with your information

  • It is never sold, rented or traded.
  • It is never shared for advertising or given to data brokers.
  • It is not used to build profiles or to make automated decisions about you.
  • It is not used to send marketing you did not ask for.

Information is disclosed to others only where the law requires it, or to the service providers named in this policy that are necessary to deliver a message to its recipient.

How long information is kept

  • Instagram message text — not retained by the automated service at all.
  • Instagram user ID — in temporary memory for at most seven days, and erased on restart.
  • Enquiry emails — kept in the email inbox while they are needed for the booking, and deleted on request.

How to have your data deleted

You can ask for your information to be deleted at any time, and you do not need to give a reason.

Email giancarlorrv@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request", and include the email address you used on the form or the Instagram handle you messaged from, so the right records can be found.

Requests are actioned within 30 days, and you will receive a confirmation once it is done.

You may also remove this service's access to your Instagram data yourself, at any time, from Instagram → Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request a copy of the personal information held about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, and to object to or restrict how it is used. To exercise any of these, contact giancarlorrv@gmail.com.

Children

This website and the automated messaging service are not directed at children under 13, and information is not knowingly collected from them. If a child has sent information, contact the address above and it will be deleted.

Security

Messages are transmitted over encrypted connections, and every incoming request to the automated service is cryptographically verified as genuinely coming from Meta before it is processed. Credentials are held as environment variables on the hosting provider and are not published in the website's source code. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date above.
